# Writing Samples

Paste 2-3 of your own past pieces below. This file is the demonstrated half of the voice reference; the declared half lives in `voice-profile-template.md`.

**How to choose samples**:
- Each sample shows the voice in a different mode (e.g., long-form + column, or case-study + thought-leadership)
- At least one sample from the past 12 months
- Published final versions only, not drafts
- Your own writing — never AI-generated, never heavily ghost-edited
- Do not include more than 3 samples; 4+ turns the reference into noise

**How to annotate**: for each sample, record observed NNGroup axis values, brand voice axis values, and 3-5 bullets on what you want preserved. Observed values may differ from the declared profile — the gap is useful signal.
